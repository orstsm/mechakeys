# MechaKeys 2.13.0

## New sound profiles

- Adds Holy Panda, MX Blue, MX Brown, NK Cream and Typewriter as included sound profiles.
- Keeps every profile preloaded in the existing 48 kHz polyphonic audio engine.
- Supports distinct Space, Delete/Backspace and Enter recordings when supplied by a profile.
- Supports optional press-and-release playback for the included profiles that contain release recordings.

## Audio rights

- The five new profiles are included by private permission from their rights holder.
- The recordings are not licensed for extraction, reuse, repackaging or separate redistribution.

## Preserved behavior

- Preserves 25 ms stale-event protection, 30-second idle suspension, event-driven notch hover, Bluetooth pause, microphone pause and universal Intel/Apple Silicon support.
