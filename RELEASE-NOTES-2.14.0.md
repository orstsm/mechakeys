# MechaKeys 2.14.0

- Switch between menu-bar controls and the island with “Use Menu Bar Instead of Island” in either interface. The choice persists across launches.
- Island hover monitoring stops in menu-bar mode.
- Opening requires a continuous 180 ms hover inside the physical notch. Passing across or dragging over it cancels opening.
- Smooth panel expansion and collapse respect Reduce Motion. The collapsed and closing panel passes clicks through to other apps.
- Preserves the existing audio engine, sound profiles and idle suspension.
